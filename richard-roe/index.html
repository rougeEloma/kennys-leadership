Richard Roe runs operations for Kenny's International. He makes sure the group's stores in Kinshasa, Lubumbashi, Kolwezi and Pointe-Noire are stocked, staffed and ready, and he oversees the sites that host them, including K-Galerie.

Areas of focus

Richard Roe

  • Store operations

    Sets the standards every store follows, from opening routines to visual presentation and security.

  • Supply & logistics

    Coordinates shipments from the houses to Kinshasa and Pointe-Noire, and keeps stock moving between stores.

  • Sites & projects

    Oversees K-Galerie and new store fit-outs, from the first plans to the day the doors open.
